PHAROS — Under Construction

The PHAROS spectral catalogue is currently under development.

The PHAROS documentation and user guide are available at:

https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2611.19734

The PHAROS manuscript has been submitted to Astronomy & Astrophysics (A&A). The stellar spectral energy distributions (SEDs) will be made publicly available following acceptance of the manuscript.

In the meantime, individual spectra are available upon request. If you need access to a specific stellar spectrum, please contact:
